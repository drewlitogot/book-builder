# Content outline

**Who this book is for:** anyone trying Book Builder for the first time. This is a small sample book
that ships with the app so every screen has something to show. Replace it with your own book, or
create a new one from the Library.

## The through-line

| Part | The question it answers | Hand-off to the next part |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Planning | "What is my book about?" | "Once the plan is clear, start writing." |
| 2. Writing | "How do I turn the plan into chapters?" | "Build, review and publish." |

## Part 1: Planning (`01-planning`)

1. **Choosing a topic**: picking a subject and a reader; writing a one-sentence promise
2. **Making an outline**: parts, chapters and the order readers need them in

## Part 2: Writing (`02-writing`)

3. **Drafting chapters**: research notes, a first draft, and where AI assistance fits
4. **Revising and approving**: reviews, checks, and marking a chapter approved
