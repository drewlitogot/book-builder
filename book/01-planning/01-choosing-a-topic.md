# Choosing a topic

This is a sample chapter. It exists so you can see how Book Builder shows an approved chapter in
the Library, in the reader website and in every output format.

## Start with the reader

A book starts with a reader. Name them in a few words: who they are, what they already know and
what they're stuck on. Every later choice gets easier once the reader is clear.

## Make one promise

Write one sentence that says what the reader will be able to do after reading the book. Keep that
sentence where you can see it while you plan, and cut anything that doesn't serve it [@sample2026].

::: {.neurotype for="group-a" title="If you have too many ideas"}
Write each idea on its own line, then keep only the ones that serve the promise. The others can
become a second book.
:::

## Try it

- Name the reader in a few words.
- Finish the sentence: "After reading this book, you will be able to…"
- List three questions the reader would ask first.
